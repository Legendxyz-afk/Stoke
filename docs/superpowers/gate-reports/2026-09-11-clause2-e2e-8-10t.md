## 2. The stopping rule, and what cut it

After pair 3 the rule was fixed, before any more data: **two more pairs, grade
on all five.** Pair 4's stoke unit banked (85.01 s); its llama unit was starting
when the harness **killed the background job for low system memory** (15.6 GB
box, Chrome open, `llama-server` at a 7.3 GB working set). No server or driver
process was left behind (checked before touching anything); the bank kept every
completed unit. The grade is therefore on 3 complete pairs plus one extra stoke
unit — included in stoke's median, excluded from the per-pair table — and the
deviation from the stopping rule is external, not a look at the numbers.

⚠ DURABLE, for the record and for [[instrument-likelier-broken]]: **this harness
terminates background jobs when the box runs low on memory, silently from the
job's point of view.** That is the shape of the 2026-08-26 "killed three times
mid-run with no user message" (standing rule 2). A model-server unit on this
box needs Chrome closed or a foreground slice.

  than tonight's box had free.
