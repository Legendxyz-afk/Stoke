3 cycles, point-order and arm-order both rotating, 6 threads, n=128. One binary; `STOKE_ATT_GQA=0` is M4.5's q-head-outer loop. Both arms verified byte-identical to the archives (`run_m5_e2_verify.ps1`), so this is speed only. Affinity **PINNED 0xFFF (P-core siblings)** over 16 logical processors.

## Against the pre-registered prediction

1. **Argued 1.03x / 1.18x / 1.44x at ctx 87 / 554 / 1500; priced at half, 1.015x / 1.09x / 1.22x.** Derived by dividing the measured slope's byte count by gqa_group and leaving the intercept alone. A number near 1.44x would be the FIRST kernel argument here to land at full value and should be treated as suspicious until re-run, not as vindication.
2. **The SHAPE matters more than the headline.** E2 removes a term proportional to context; the weight term it leaves alone is constant. A flat gain across p00/p40/long refutes the mechanism even if the totals look good.
3. **N=8 is the risk point.** The unit count per sequence falls 32 -> 8, so a straggler holds 4x more work at the barrier. A regression is expected here before anywhere else, and STOKE_ATT_CHUNK is the knob if one appears.
