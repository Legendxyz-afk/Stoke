# L4 measured: the pool was the whole win, and the prediction ran hot again

2026-08-18, Opus 5, unattended chain leg 2. Report:
`docs/superpowers/gate-reports/2026-08-18-m45-att-par-ab-report.md`, raw log
beside it. 3 cycles, point- and arm-order both rotating, t=8 unpinned, n=128,
`--kv f32`, llama at matched depth. All four arms are byte-identical (10/10
gate, `a48de9f`), so this is speed only.

## 1. What it says

| point | ctx | par0/lanes1 | par1/lanes4 | gain | llama | B/L |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| p00 | 87 | 13.896 | 15.170 | 1.092× | 18.32 | 0.828 |
| p40 | 554 | 8.028 | 12.683 | 1.580× | 16.28 | 0.779 |
| long | 1500 | 3.185 | 9.368 | **2.941×** | 12.77 | 0.734 |

**The slope, which is the number that mattered:** 0.1713 → **0.0289**
ms/context-token, a 5.9× reduction, against llama's 0.0168. Stoke was 11.9×
worse than llama on slope; it is now **1.72× worse**.

## 2. The prediction is graded: right direction, ~1.7× too generous

The 08-17 note predicted ctx-1500 decode **317 → ~63 ms** and attention
**268 → ~14 ms**. Measured: **313.97 → 106.75 ms**. The win is real and large,
and it is not the predicted win — attention landed near ~58 ms, not ~14.

At P-BAR's own configuration the miss is worse. The note predicted ~50 ms/token
at ctx 87 (≈20 tok/s against llama's 18.5, ≈1.06 B/L). Measured **65.92 ms,
15.17 tok/s, 0.828 B/L**.

**This is the third time a products-per-instruction argument of this shape has
run hot** — A′ predicted a win and measured 0.907×; Q6_K predicted 1.20× "as a
floor" and measured 1.108×; L4 predicted 5.0× on decode and measured 2.94×. The
pattern is now strong enough to be a rule: *an arithmetic argument about a
kernel predicts the DIRECTION reliably and the MAGNITUDE ~1.5–2× hot.* Price
future rungs at half their argued gain.

## 3. The finding nobody predicted: the lanes are redundant

Separating the two changes at ctx 1500 (ms/token):

| arm | ms/tok | what it isolates |
|---|---|---|
| par0/lanes1 | 313.97 | pre-L4 |
| par0/lanes4 | 249.25 | interleaved QK loop alone — 1.26× |
| par1/lanes1 | 108.10 | heads on the pool alone — **2.90×** |
| par1/lanes4 | 106.75 | both — 2.94× |

**The pool alone is 99% of the win, and the lanes add 1.3% on top of it.** The
two changes were attacking the same stall: a dependent FP-add chain that leaves
the machine idle. Parallelising the heads already fills the machine, after which
filling the pipeline within one head buys almost nothing.

`ATT_LANES = 4` is therefore carrying its complexity for ~1%. It is bit-exact
and already shipped, so nothing is urgent, but it is now a simplification
candidate rather than an optimisation — and the 08-17 research that justified
the number (two FP-add ports, 2–3 cycle latency) explained a real effect that
the pool then made almost irrelevant.

## 4. Efficiency, since parallel wins can be bought with waste

At ctx 1500 the pooled arm spends 797 CPU-seconds against the serial arm's 679
— **1.17× the processor time for 2.94× the wall speed.** The serial arm was
leaving seven cores idle; this is close to free.

## 5. Consequences

**The M5 alarm is retired.** "Extrapolated to ctx 1024 stoke pays ~187 ms/token
(~5.4 tok/s) vs llama ~61, = 0.33×, so M5's aggregate bars are unreachable" was
measured against a single-threaded attention loop. On the new slope, ctx 1024
costs **~93 ms/token (~10.8 tok/s)** against llama's ~70 ms (~14.2), a ratio of
**0.76×, not 0.33×**. With batching amortising the weight term, N=8 aggregate
has room against the 17.7 bar (the traffic ceiling at that point is 81.7).
**M5's bars are back in range.** The 08-11 budget's structural claim — M5 is a
KV-traffic problem, crossover N×L ≈ 16,936 — is untouched and now the live
constraint.

**P-BAR, however, is still missed at p00.** 0.828 against the 0.85 bar, which is
where it already sat before L4 (0.827–0.838). L4 buys 9.2% at ctx 87 and the bar
does not move, because at ctx 87 attention is only ~24% of the token and the
milestone's bar is graded exactly there. **A long-context win does not close a
short-context bar.** The two P-BAR legs running tonight grade this at their own
configurations; this note does not pre-empt them.

## 6. One discrepancy to resolve before quoting anything absolute

Tonight's `par0/lanes1` at p00 reads **13.896** tok/s. The 2026-08-17 P-BAR at
t=8 unpinned measured what should be the same code path at **15.492** — 10%
higher — while llama moved only 2% between the two sittings (18.32 vs 18.70).
A box-state effect should depress both arms, not one.

Candidate explanations, none confirmed: the L4 commit added a per-head pointer
indirection that the serial path also pays; the `g_att_min_pos` branch costs
something at ctx 87; or the two sittings differ in a way llama is insensitive to.
**Leg 4 resolves it directly** — it runs arm A (pre-maddubs) against its own
9.989/10.835 anchors, so if arm A reproduces, the box was fine and HEAD's p00
number is real. Until then, quote the RATIOS from this sitting and not the
absolute rates.
