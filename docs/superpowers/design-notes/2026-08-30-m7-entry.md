## 2. Inherited from M6, in the order M6 found them

1. **The scheduler's cross-thread surface.** `sched.h` says the submit queue is
   the ONE place a foreign thread touches the scheduler; `stoke.h` — now FROZEN
   — promises `stoke_poll` and `stoke_cancel` are safe from any thread. They
   reach `find()`, which walks `s->all` unlocked, while the step thread prepends
   to it unlocked in `drain_submits`. Benign on x86, real on weak memory.
2. **The first-token ordering.** A request submitted mid-step can have its first
   token chosen greedily. Documented in `stoke_submit`; closing it means
   draining at admission, which is inside the scheduler.
3. **The M5 bars' ABSOLUTES**, still M5's. A6's rate half was discharged by an
   A/B with a null arm, not by re-taking them.
4. **The Linux C++ TU box** — `apt install g++` on the WSL side.
5. **`stoke run` has no sampling flags.**

## 3. Rung order, and why

### B1 — the cross-thread surface ← FIRST
**Because the frozen ABI already promises it**, and because it turns out to be
cheap: `sched.h` injects the model step and the clock through `SchedHost`, so
`test_sched.c` drives the REAL scheduler with a fake model and no GGUF at all.

⚠⚠ **That is what makes this rung possible.** M6 recorded that the race is "not
gradeable on this box" because TSan OOMs on anything model-heavy (`abi_smoke`
peaks at 7,364,440 kB against 7 GB of WSL). A SIM-driven test has no model in
it, so TSan can watch submit, step and poll running on three threads at once —
which is precisely the configuration M6 noted no test exercises, and therefore
precisely why its clean TSan result never covered this.

Shape: prove it first (a test that makes TSan report), then lock the all-list
(`find`, `drain_submits`, the `sched_submit` duplicate check), then the same
test green.

### B2 — the bars' absolutes, on a quiet box
Before more scheduler work, not after. M7 owes a REPRODUCIBLE benchmark report
([5]), the absolutes are still M5's, and B1/B3 both touch the scheduler — so a
current baseline is worth more taken now than reconstructed later.
⚠ Needs the quiesce bar (two consecutive `_Total` reads ≤ 12%). The box failed
it on 2026-08-30 at median 14.5% even after stopping ISLC.
▶ The A/B reference for any M7 scheduler work is tag **`m6`**, the way `m5` was
for A6; `tests/tooling/run_a6_rate_ab.sh` is the runner and takes a binary path.

### B3 — the first-token ordering

## 5. Standing constraints this milestone inherits

Unchanged from M6, and the two that bit most recently are worth repeating:

- **Any perf claim needs a same-sitting, one-binary A/B with a null arm**, and
  the null arm is only a denominator at its OWN level.
- **Every bank keyed by the binary.**
- **Gate sittings foreground, sliced one unit per invocation**, each unit
  carrying its own quiesce flag.
- **Build and test from bash, not PowerShell**; on the WSL leg, check every
  artifact is an ELF before believing a red, and remember that
  `wsl.exe -- bash -c '…'` eats `$var`.
- **`stoke.h` IS FROZEN.** Error codes are never renumbered, fields are only
  appended, nothing is removed. A change a caller could notice bumps
  STOKE_ABI_VERSION.
